# Yapper privacy policy

Published at https://goldpenguin.org/yapper/privacy/ (Gold Penguin LLC). Keep the two in step.

_Last updated: 25 September 2026_

Yapper is a voice dictation app for iPhone. It is built so that nothing you say or write leaves your iPhone.

## What we collect

Nothing. Yapper has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. We never receive
your audio, your text or anything about how you use the app.

## What stays on your iPhone

- **Audio.** Yapper records only while you dictate. Speech is turned into text on your iPhone, by NVIDIA's Parakeet
  model or by Apple's speech recognizer forced to run on the device. Each recording is kept on your iPhone for 1 day
  by default so you can play it back or transcribe it again. You can change that in Settings › Keep recordings,
  from Don't keep to Forever. Recordings are left out of iCloud backups.
- **Text.** Your dictations, including failed attempts, are kept in History on your iPhone for as long as you choose
  in Settings › Keep text. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud, iOS includes app data such as History in that backup,
  as it does for any app.
- **Clipboard.** If Copy every dictation is on, each dictation is placed on the clipboard of this iPhone only. It is
  not shared with your other devices.
- **Stats.** Daily word and dictation counts are stored on your iPhone to show your stats.

You can delete any dictation, or all of History, in the app. Deleting the app deletes everything.

## The keyboard and Full Access

iOS keyboards cannot use the microphone. The Yapper keyboard asks the Yapper app to listen, and the two pass your
text through a private App Group folder on your iPhone. That is the only reason Yapper asks for Full Access. The
keyboard contains no network code. Without Full Access, the keyboard still types; it just can't dictate.

## The internet

The only network request Yapper makes is downloading the speech model, which happens only when you tap Download.
The files come from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which, like any website, sees your device's IP address when it
serves them. After that, Yapper works fully offline.

## Permissions

- **Microphone:** to record while you dictate.
- **Speech Recognition:** only to use Apple's on-device recognizer, when you choose it or before Parakeet is ready.

You can turn either off at any time in iOS Settings › Yapper.

## Children

Yapper collects no data from anyone, including children.
